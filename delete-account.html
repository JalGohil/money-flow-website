Account Deletion

Last updated: November 21, 2026

At Money Flow, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. If you no longer wish to use Money Flow, you can contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Money Flow account, follow these steps:

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Send an email to our support address below.
  3. Use the subject line "Money Flow Account Deletion Request".
  4. Include the email address associated with your Money Flow account.
  5. Wait for our response if additional information is needed to verify your request.

Request Account Deletion

Email Deletion Request

2. Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the information associated with your account and the services you have used, an approved deletion request may include:

  • Your Money Flow account and associated authentication information.
  • Your account email address and profile information held by the service.
  • Financial records stored in supported cloud services, such as income, expenses and transactions.
  • Cloud-stored budgets, savings goals and account information.
  • Other personal information associated with your cloud account, where applicable.

3. Information That May Need Separate Deletion

Some information may be stored outside your Money Flow cloud account. Account deletion may not automatically remove:

  • Information stored locally on your phone or other device.
  • Backup copies you have saved separately in Google Drive.
  • Copies of information you have exported or saved elsewhere.
  • Information that must be retained where required by applicable law.

You may need to remove these separate copies yourself. Uninstalling the application alone should not be treated as confirmation that your cloud account has been deleted.

4. Verification and Processing

We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing a deletion request. This helps protect users against unauthorised deletion requests.

We aim to review requests and process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to verification, technical requirements and applicable legal obligations.

We may retain limited information when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or protect the security of our services.

5. What Happens After Deletion?

Once an account deletion request has been verified and completed, you may no longer be able to sign in to that account or access cloud information associated with it.

Deletion may be irreversible. If you want to keep your financial records, make any necessary backups before requesting deletion.
