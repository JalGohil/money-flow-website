Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Money Flow ("we", "our" or "the app") is a personal finance application designed to help users manage income, expenses, budgets, accounts and financial goals.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Money Flow.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Money Flow may handle:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication information.
  • Profile information, such as your display name.
  • Financial records you enter, including income, expenses, transactions, budgets, savings goals, accounts and related notes.
  • App preferences, such as your selected currency, appearance and accent colour.
  • Information contained in backup or synchronisation files that you choose to create or restore.

2. How Information Is Stored

Money Flow stores financial records locally on your device using its local database.

If you choose to use cloud synchronisation, selected account and financial information may be transferred to and stored using Supabase services.

Cloud synchronisation is a user-initiated feature. Financial records are not automatically uploaded after every transaction.

Local records and cloud records may be stored separately. Removing the app or signing out does not necessarily remove an existing cloud copy.

3. Account Authentication

Money Flow uses Supabase authentication to support account registration and sign-in.

Depending on the sign-in method you select, this may involve email and password authentication or Google sign-in.

When you use Google sign-in, Google processes your authentication information under its own privacy policy. Money Flow receives the account information needed to authenticate you.

4. Google Drive Backup

Money Flow provides Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

If you choose to use this feature, the app may access the Google Drive permissions required to create or restore your selected backup.

Backup information is used to provide the backup and restore functionality requested by you. We do not use Google user data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Google's use of information obtained through its services is also governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. How We Use Information

Information handled by Money Flow is used to:

  • Provide account registration and authentication.
  • Display and organise your financial records.
  • Calculate financial summaries and reports.
  • Manage budgets, accounts and savings goals.
  • Provide cloud synchronisation when requested.
  • Provide backup and restore functionality.
  • Respond to support requests.

6. Sharing of Information

Money Flow uses service providers, including Supabase and Google services, to provide the functionality described in this policy.

Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to deliver the features you choose to use.

We do not sell your personal information or financial records.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Money Flow. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should protect your device, account credentials and app lock PIN. Do not share your passwords or PIN with others.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local financial records may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the application.

Cloud-stored information may remain in your account until it is deleted through the applicable service or a deletion request is processed.

To request deletion of your Money Flow account and associated cloud information, contact us at:

moneyflowapp.support@gmail.com

Please note that deleting cloud information does not automatically delete local copies or separate Google Drive backups. Those may need to be deleted separately.

9. Children's Privacy

Money Flow is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Money Flow develops. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact:

Money Flow Support
Email: moneyflowapp.support@gmail.com
